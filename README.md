# Applied Math

Mathematical foundations and computational implementations from an MS in Applied and Computational Mathematics at the University of Washington. Each module covers a distinct area of applied mathematics, combining rigorous theoretical derivation with practical implementation and empirical analysis.

## Modules

### [`computational-data-analysis-methods/`](./computational-data-analysis-methods/)
Five studies from AMATH 582 spanning signal processing, computer vision, and machine learning: 3D Fourier analysis for submarine tracking, adaptive wavelet edge detection, randomized SVD for video background subtraction, kernel ridge regression for classification, and spectral clustering via graph Laplacian embeddings.

### [`optimization/`](./optimization/)
Two computational studies from AMATH 515: empirical convergence analysis of steepest descent, Newton, DFP, and BFGS with Wolfe line search on convex and non-convex objectives; and image recovery via proximal gradient descent and FISTA with full derivation of the L1 proximal operator and DCT gradient.
